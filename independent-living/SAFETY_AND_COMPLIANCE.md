# Safety, Privacy & Compliance Guardrails

This is a planning checklist, not legal advice or proof of compliance. Requirements must be checked for each province/territory and service category before launch.

## Transportation
- Start with non-emergency transportation only.
- Confirm applicable commercial vehicle, passenger transportation, accessible vehicle, driver, insurance, and municipal/provincial requirements.
- Collect only trip details needed to quote and coordinate the ride.
- Establish safe pickup/drop-off, mobility-aid securement, no-show, cancellation, and incident processes.
- Never imply drivers provide medical supervision unless qualified and explicitly contracted to do so.

## Companionship and household assistance
- Define permitted tasks and prohibited personal/clinical care clearly.
- Establish identity, reference, background-screening, insurance, training, and safeguarding standards appropriate to the service and jurisdiction.
- Provide a clear process for complaints, suspected abuse, missing persons, and immediate danger; emergency situations must be directed to local emergency services.
- Do not market independent providers as employees or as “certified” without evidence.

## Family coordination and consent
- An adult receiving the service remains the decision-maker unless a lawful representative is established.
- Obtain explicit, revocable authorization before a family member books on another adult's behalf or receives booking/status information.
- Record who granted consent, its scope, date, and revocation.
- Avoid exposing addresses, schedules, or sensitive details to unauthorized family members.

## Privacy and security
- Collect the minimum needed to fulfil a request.
- Avoid diagnoses, medication lists, health-card numbers, and detailed medical histories in the initial MVP.
- Use secure authentication, least-privilege roles, row-level security, audit logs, retention/deletion rules, and encrypted transport/storage.
- Never put service-role keys or payment secrets in browser code or source control.
- Define breach response, vendor access, data processing, and cross-border hosting disclosures.

## Accessibility and customer experience
- Design to WCAG 2.2 AA as a target.
- Support keyboard navigation, screen readers, high contrast, scalable text, plain language, and mobile use.
- Offer phone-assisted booking as an operational alternative for people who cannot use the website.

## Funding information
- Show source, jurisdiction, eligibility conditions, last-checked date, and official application link.
- Describe possible programs as information, not guaranteed benefits.
- Never submit an application or make eligibility claims on a person's behalf without explicit authorization and accurate supporting information.

## Pre-launch legal review
Obtain province-specific advice on privacy, consumer protection, accessibility, transportation, employment/contractor classification, vulnerable-sector screening, insurance, payments, tax, and marketplace liability.
