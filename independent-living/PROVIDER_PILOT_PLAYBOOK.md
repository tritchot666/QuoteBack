# Manitoba Pilot Playbook

Status: operational plan. No providers have been recruited or verified yet.

## Pilot goal

Validate whether families and older adults will use and pay for help coordinating non-emergency rides and everyday assistance, and whether local providers will reliably respond to referrals.

Do not start with all service categories. Begin with:
1. Non-emergency, pre-booked rides (including accessible vehicles where available).
2. Errands and grocery assistance.
3. Companionship / friendly visits.
4. Light household assistance.

Home modifications can be added as a quote-request category after the initial matching workflow is proven.

## Initial geography

Start in one Winnipeg service area. Do not advertise province-wide coverage until real provider capacity is confirmed. Add Brandon and other Manitoba communities only after the first area has reliable coverage.

## Provider recruitment

Recruit established local operators first, then independent providers where appropriate:
- Accessible transportation companies and community transportation organizations.
- Non-emergency passenger transportation providers.
- Home-support and housekeeping businesses.
- Errand and grocery-support providers.
- Reputable companionship and community-support organizations.

For every provider, record:
- Legal/business name and accountable contact.
- Services actually offered and excluded tasks.
- Service area and operating hours.
- Pricing method and cancellation/no-show policy.
- Relevant licences/permits where applicable.
- Insurance type, insurer, policy expiry and review date.
- Driver credentials and vehicle accessibility details for transportation.
- Identity, references, background-screening and safeguarding checks appropriate to role and law.
- Complaint and incident contact.
- Agreement to privacy, conduct, accessibility and customer communication standards.

Never describe a provider as verified, vetted, insured, licensed, or accessible unless the specific evidence has been checked and recorded. Verification expires and must be renewed.

## Family/customer discovery

Interview at least:
- 10 family caregivers coordinating support remotely.
- 5 older adults or people with disabilities who arrange their own services.
- 10 local service providers.

Ask about recent real experiences:
- What service did you need most recently?
- How did you find someone?
- How long did it take?
- What was confusing, stressful, or unsafe?
- What did you pay, and who paid?
- What would make you trust a matching service?
- Would you pay a booking fee or monthly family membership? At what price?
- What would make you stop using it?

Do not lead respondents with the product pitch before understanding their current behaviour.

## Concierge pilot workflow

1. Customer submits a request or calls for assisted booking.
2. Operator confirms service is non-emergency and within pilot scope.
3. Confirm the person receiving service understands and agrees to the request. If a family member is coordinating, record explicit, scoped, revocable authorization.
4. Operator contacts only providers whose service, geography, and checks match the request.
5. Share minimum necessary information; disclose the customer's contact details only at the appropriate stage.
6. Present availability, price, scope, limitations, and relevant credentials clearly.
7. Customer chooses provider and accepts terms directly.
8. Confirm booking, cancellation process, and who to contact if plans change.
9. Follow up after service and record feedback/complaints.

## Pilot success gates

Do not scale based on signups alone. Track:
- Number of genuine requests.
- Percentage matched with at least one suitable provider.
- Median time to first suitable response.
- Booking conversion rate.
- Completed-service rate.
- Cancellation and no-show rates.
- Repeat request rate within 60 days.
- Customer trust/satisfaction.
- Provider response and acceptance rates.
- Gross margin per completed booking, including operator time and support costs.
- Incidents and complaints (target is not zero reporting; target is safe handling and transparent resolution).

Suggested validation gate before substantial automation:
- 15 customer/family interviews completed.
- 10 provider interviews completed.
- At least 5 providers willing to participate in a controlled pilot.
- At least 5 genuine requests handled.
- At least 3 paid pilot bookings or other demonstrated willingness to pay.
- No unresolved high-severity safety or privacy issue.

## Marketing and outreach rules

- Be transparent that the platform is in a pilot and coverage is limited.
- Do not imply 24/7 availability, guaranteed rides, clinical support, or emergency response.
- Do not scrape private contact details or send unsolicited bulk messages.
- Use compliant Canadian anti-spam practices and honour opt-outs.
- Build partnerships with community organizations rather than presenting them as endorsers without permission.
