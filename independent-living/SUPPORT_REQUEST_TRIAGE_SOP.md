# Support request triage and referral procedure

**Pilot:** Winnipeg, Manitoba  
**Scope:** Non-emergency, non-clinical independent-living supports only.  
**Status:** Manual operating procedure draft; use only after privacy, legal and insurance review.

## 1. Intake acknowledgement
1. Confirm the request is within the published service area and supported categories.
2. Confirm the adult receiving the service knows about and agrees to the request.
3. If a family member or other supporter is involved, record the adult's explicit permission and the exact role permitted. Do not assume family relationship creates authority.
4. Explain that the platform coordinates introductions and does not guarantee provider availability or service outcomes.
5. Do not request diagnoses, medication details, health-card numbers or medical records.

## 2. Immediate exclusion and escalation
Stop ordinary intake and direct the person to emergency services if they describe an immediate threat to life or safety. Do not attempt to dispatch a provider as an emergency response.

Do not accept requests for ambulance/stretcher transport, clinical assessment, nursing, medication administration, regulated personal care or other regulated services unless a separately reviewed and authorized service pathway exists. For uncertainty, pause and escalate to the designated operations lead; do not improvise.

## 3. Clarify practical requirements
Collect only what is necessary:
- Service category and plain-language task description.
- Requested date/time window and flexibility.
- General service area; obtain a precise address only when needed for a specific referral and with consent.
- Practical accessibility logistics relevant to the task (for example, step-free access, mobility device space, assistance boundaries).
- Preferred contact method and safe contact times.
- Any task boundary or limitation the provider must know before accepting.

Do not collect unnecessary sensitive details. Never promise a provider can perform a transfer, lift or other physical assistance without verified capability and explicit scope.

## 4. Determine whether a safe match exists
1. Search only providers approved for the exact category and service area.
2. Check that insurance and required credentials remain current.
3. Compare scope, availability, price, travel fees and cancellation terms.
4. Share the minimum necessary request details only with providers specifically selected for the referral.
5. Record what was shared, with whom, when and under what consent.
6. If no suitable provider exists, tell the customer plainly; do not substitute an unverified provider or imply coverage.

## 5. Present options and confirm
Provide the customer with the provider's identity, service scope, availability, full quoted price and relevant limitations. The customer chooses whether to proceed. Confirm date, time, location, price, cancellation terms and contact method in writing. A provider response is not a booking until both parties have explicitly confirmed.

## 6. Completion and follow-up
After the scheduled service, ask whether it occurred and whether there are concerns. Record completion status and operational feedback without collecting unnecessary personal details. Provide a clear channel for complaints and incidents.

## 7. Incident, complaint or consent change
- Prioritize immediate safety and emergency response where appropriate.
- Pause the affected referral/provider if a credible safety concern arises.
- Notify the designated operations lead promptly.
- Record factual observations, times, actions and people notified; avoid speculation.
- Preserve only necessary records with restricted access.
- Follow the reviewed incident response, insurer notification and legal reporting procedures.
- If consent is withdrawn, stop future sharing and referrals based on that consent; review any information already disclosed and document the response.

## 8. Minimum request statuses
- **Received:** request submitted; not yet reviewed.
- **Needs clarification:** waiting for customer response; no provider disclosure.
- **Screening:** operator checks service fit and consent.
- **Seeking provider:** limited referral sent to approved providers.
- **Options sent:** customer has received provider responses.
- **Confirmed:** customer and provider explicitly accepted the booking.
- **Completed:** service completion confirmed.
- **Cancelled:** request or booking cancelled; reason recorded where appropriate.
- **Unable to match:** no safe, suitable provider found.
- **Incident review:** affected workflow paused pending review.

## Operator rule
When safety, consent, scope or provider eligibility is unclear, pause the request. A missed booking is preferable to an unsafe or unauthorized referral.
