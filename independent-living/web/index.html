Accessible transportation

Non-emergency rides to appointments, shopping and community activities. Mobility arrangements are discussed before any booking.

Companionship

Friendly visits, social outings and scheduled check-ins to help people stay connected.

🛒

Errands and groceries

Help with shopping, deliveries, returns and other everyday tasks.

🧹

Household assistance

Light housekeeping, laundry and other practical non-clinical help at home.

Connect with contractors for ramps, grab bars, safer bathrooms and other modifications.
