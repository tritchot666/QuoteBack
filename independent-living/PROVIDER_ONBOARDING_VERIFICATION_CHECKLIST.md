# Provider onboarding and verification checklist

**Pilot area:** Winnipeg, Manitoba  
**Status:** Operational draft — not legal advice and not evidence any provider has been verified.

## 1. Before collecting an application
Explain that applying does not guarantee approval, referrals, work or payment. Explain what information is collected, why it is needed, who can see it, how long it is retained and how to request access/correction/deletion. Collect only business and service information needed for screening.

## 2. Identity and business checks
- [ ] Legal name and operating/business name recorded.
- [ ] Primary contact identity verified using a proportionate, secure process.
- [ ] Business registration or applicable operating status checked where relevant.
- [ ] Service address and service-area boundaries recorded.
- [ ] References contacted and outcomes dated.
- [ ] Complaints, relevant enforcement history or material safety concerns discussed fairly and consistently.
- [ ] Written provider agreement accepted.

## 3. Insurance, credentials and category-specific checks
- [ ] Current insurance certificate reviewed; coverage, exclusions, limits and expiry recorded.
- [ ] Required licences, permits, registrations or training independently checked with the issuing authority.
- [ ] Background screening selected based on role, applicable law and a documented risk assessment; do not request or retain unnecessary sensitive details.
- [ ] Transportation: verify driver authorization, vehicle registration/inspection where required, commercial-use coverage, accessibility equipment and local requirements.
- [ ] Home modifications: verify trade qualifications, permits, liability coverage and applicable safety requirements.
- [ ] Errands/household support: define cash, keys, valuables, privacy, boundaries and lone-worker procedures.
- [ ] Companionship: define visit boundaries, safeguarding, check-in/out and escalation procedures.

## 4. Service scope and customer-facing information
- [ ] Exact services offered and excluded documented.
- [ ] Areas, hours, lead times and capacity documented.
- [ ] Pricing method, minimum charges, travel fees and cancellation terms documented.
- [ ] Accessibility limitations and equipment details described accurately.
- [ ] Emergency and clinical needs explicitly excluded from this non-clinical pilot.
- [ ] Provider agrees not to make medical, funding eligibility or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- [ ] Provider agrees to notify the platform immediately of incidents, complaints, insurance changes or credential expiry.

## 5. Approval decision
An operator records one outcome:
- **Pending:** evidence incomplete; no referrals.
- **Approved for specified categories:** all required checks complete; only approved scope may be referred.
- **Declined:** reason recorded in a restricted internal record; communicate respectfully.
- **Suspended:** referrals paused pending review of an incident, complaint, expired document or changed circumstances.

Approval must be made by an authorized operator, never by the provider's own account. Set a review date and credential expiry reminders. Re-check before renewal and after material incidents.

## 6. Referral readiness
- [ ] Provider profile visible only to authorized operators.
- [ ] Only approved service categories and geographic areas can be matched.
- [ ] Provider has accepted privacy, confidentiality and safeguarding requirements.
- [ ] Test referral completed without exposing unnecessary customer information.
- [ ] Operator knows how to pause referrals immediately.

## Recordkeeping
Store the verification outcome, verifier, date, evidence type, issuing body, expiry date and next review date. Avoid retaining full identity documents or background-check reports unless a documented legal necessity exists. Restrict access and define deletion dates.

**Do not describe a provider as “certified,” “licensed,” “insured” or “background checked” unless the exact claim has been independently verified and is current.**
