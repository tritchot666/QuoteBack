Everyday support, all in one place

Explore the types of local services the platform is being designed to connect people with. Availability and provider coverage will depend on location.

🚐

Accessible transportation

Non-emergency rides to appointments, shopping, community activities and visits—with mobility needs discussed in advance.

🤝

Companionship

Friendly visits, social outings and scheduled check-ins to help people stay connected.

🛒

Errands & groceries

Help with shopping, deliveries, returns and other everyday tasks.

🧹

Household assistance

Light housekeeping, laundry, meal preparation and practical non-clinical help at home.

🛠️

Home accessibility

Connect with contractors for ramps, grab bars, safer bathrooms, stair solutions and other modifications.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family coordination

Help authorized family members organize services and stay informed—with the person's permission.

For families and caregivers

Helping someone from another city?

The vision is to let families coordinate practical support for a loved one, with that person's clear permission and control over their information.

Our safety approach
Important: This is an early concept prototype. It does not accept service requests, verify providers, dispatch rides, provide emergency or medical services, or store personal information. For emergencies, contact local emergency services. Provider screening, insurance, privacy, accessibility and provincial requirements must be in place before bookings open.
Independent Living Canada is a working project name, not a cleared or registered brand. Prototype only · Canada · Project notes
