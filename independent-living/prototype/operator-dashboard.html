Safe handling checklist

  • Confirm the person receiving support has agreed to the request and any family involvement.
  • Verify provider identity, references, insurance and any required licences before referrals.
  • Share only the minimum information needed for a provider to respond.
  • Keep emergency, clinical and personal-care needs outside this initial non-medical pilot.
  • Record complaints, incidents, cancellations and consent changes through an approved secure process.

No real customer records, provider approvals, payments or bookings are represented here.
